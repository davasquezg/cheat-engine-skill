[size=120][b]What's added ([Skolleks] header)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b][Resolver] SF1/SF2/SF3 bases:[/b] finds the SF1/SF2 and SF3 bases by AOB (3 signatures per base) with a static fallback. [color=#FF0000][b]Enable it first[/b][/color] — the original groups use the symbols it registers.
[*][b]SF3 — Noise Gauge locked in battle:[/b] asks for a value from 500 to 9999 (0.1% units → 50.0% to 999.9%) and keeps it for the whole battle.
[*][b]SF3 — Delete Time locked at 1:[/b] from the start of each battle until the end.
[*][b]SF3 — HP Upgrades locked:[/b] asks for a target Max HP (100–1000, default 1000 = 18 × HP+10 / 36 × HP+20) and keeps both counts locked. The two count entries inside allow manual editing (disable the script first). Max HP = 100 + 10×(+10) + 20×(+20), capped at 1000 by the game.
[*][b]SF1 — Base HP[/b] (+28, starts at 100).
[*][b]Notes verified in-game:[/b]
[list]
[*]SF1: the original "HP" (+2E) is current HP; "Max HP" (+30) is not max HP.
[*]SF1 card bytes per border: +70 white | +71 yellow | +72 blue (Mega) | +73 red (Giga).
[*]SF1 "5. All" entries are 4 bytes from +71, so they also overwrite +72/+73/+74.
[*]SF3: Max HP = 100 + 10×(HP Upgrade +10) + 20×(HP Upgrade +20), capped at 1000 (e.g. 18/36 or 30/30).
[*]SF3: the original "Mr Hertzes" entry is the Key Item count, which works as currency to buy abilities at the shop (the "Mr. Hertz saved" counter is a different address).
[/list]
